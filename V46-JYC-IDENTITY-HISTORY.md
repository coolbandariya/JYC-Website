V46 turns the public site further toward its real job: representing **JIIT Youth Club**, not becoming a student utility portal.

## Editorial rule

The history page deliberately avoids inventing a founding date or unsupported milestones. New milestones should only be added when JYC/JIIT publishes an authoritative record.

## Design rule

The page uses the existing JYC visual language: navy, ivory, champagne and restrained bronze accents, with responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. It does not introduce a student dashboard, campus utility map, attendance system, academic planner or generic social feed.

## Benchmark takeaway

Research across additional JYC/JIIT references, major Indian student festivals, university organisation ecosystems and event platforms consistently points toward:

1. clear organisation identity;
2. strong flagship-event storytelling;
3. credible leadership and governance;
4. club/community presentation with real evidence;
5. an archive that preserves institutional memory;
6. event pages that communicate the experience clearly;
7. visual storytelling that supports the brand instead of overpowering it.

V46 implements the history/identity layer first while preserving the senior-approved JYC-first public architecture.
